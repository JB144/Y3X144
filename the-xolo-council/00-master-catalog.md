# The Xolo Council — Master Catalog

Everything from the ChatGPT session and this Claude session, combined in one list (merged 2026-11-06).

**How it works:** develop each design, score it, and keep the strongest. When two designs serve the same buyer or product slot, they go head-to-head and only the winner stays. Anything that doesn't reach an ideal standard is discarded. Discarded designs move to the Cut list with a reason. Nothing is deleted.

## Scorecard (0–10 each)

| Criterion | What we're judging |
|---|---|
| Breed accuracy | Reads as a Xolo at a glance: tall upright ears, wedge muzzle slightly longer than the skull, lean rectangular body, natural tail |
| Artwork quality | Clean edges, no artifacts, correct spelling |
| Thumbnail impact | Readable at Etsy search-thumbnail size |
| Owner specificity | Something a Xolo owner recognizes, not a generic dog joke |
| Product range | How many of our products it works on: tee, 18000, 18500, mug, ornament, blanket, wall art |
| Distinctiveness | Unlike what's already on Etsy (breed-name tees, UFO dogs, generic mystical cards) |
| Brand fit | Creature Committee voice and quality |
| Print readiness | Effort needed to get it production-ready (10 = little work) |

**Tiers**
- **LEAD:** average 8.5 or higher. Launch first and build the full product set.
- **KEEP:** average 7–8.4. Launch on its best 2–3 products.
- **BENCH:** worth keeping, but blocked by a fixable problem or by timing.
- **CUT:** average below 7, lost a head-to-head, or hit an automatic reject (wrong breed or anatomy, misspelling, unfixable artifact).

## Problems that affect several designs

1. **Cream lettering.** Correction: C03, C04, C06 and C10 have a thick charcoal outline, so they stay readable on light products, just with less punch. Make a dark-fill version only if light-garment tests underperform. **C08 The Quiet Guide has no outline** and truly disappears on light backgrounds, so it must get a darker or outlined title.
2. **Resolution.** The ChatGPT images are about 1,200px wide (El Compañero is 1164×1351). Printify apparel wants about 4500×5400px at 300dpi. Each approved design needs vectorizing or a high-quality upscale, then an artifact check, before it can be printed.
3. **The coated Xolo looks like a Pharaoh Hound** (C06, and probably C09's coated figure). Fix this once and reuse the corrected coated head everywhere.
