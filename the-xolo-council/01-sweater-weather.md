## B. Customer & visual strategy

- **Buyer:** Xolo owners (mostly women, 28–55) and people buying for them. Self-gifting is common. Gift occasions: Christmas, gift exchanges, a new puppy's first Christmas.
- **The hook:** Every other breed's sweater joke is "cute." For a Xolo it's *practical*. That one joke carries the whole line.
- **Look:** cozy, warm, vintage-knit textures. Brand palette (rusty blue, coffee brown, sage) plus one **cranberry** holiday accent. Avoid generic bright red/green so the line stays on-brand.
- **Breed accuracy (every image):**
  - Large upright bat-like ears
  - Almond eyes
  - Lean, sighthound-like build
  - Smooth hairless skin with light forehead wrinkles
  - Optional small tuft on the head
  - **Not** a Chihuahua, Chinese Crested, or Pharaoh Hound

## C. Artwork designs

### XC-SW-01 — "Finally, a Reason for the Sweater"
A standard-size hairless Xolo in a chunky cable-knit holiday sweater. It sits upright, smug and dignified, with light snowfall. Text sits in an arc in vintage serif lettering.
- Main line: **FINALLY, A REASON FOR THE SWEATER**
- Small line: *The Xolo Council*
- Used on: ornament (circular crop, no small line), mug, sweater front.

### XC-SW-02 — "Xolo Fair Isle"
A seamless Fair Isle knit pattern with rows of Xolo profile silhouettes, snowflakes, pine sprigs and tiny bones. It's built as a true repeating tile.
- Used on: blanket (full repeat), mug wrap (alternate design), sweater sleeve/back if the provider supports it.
