# Botanical Relief Print

Style label: **Botanical Relief Print — El Compañero reference**.

Use the approved reference at [references/approved-el-companero.png](references/approved-el-companero.png) to understand the visual language. Inspect it before using it as an image-generation reference. It is a style reference, not a requirement to repeat its dog, wording, or exact layout.

## Visual DNA

- Original linocut/relief-print appearance: bold silhouettes, confident carved contours, tapered cream highlight cuts, and sparse internal hatching.
- A dominant recognizable subject surrounded by rhythmic flowering stems and broad leaves. Keep breathing space around the face and limbs.
- A rectangular botanical panel is the signature composition; adapt to an oval, roundel, wrap, or open arrangement when product shape benefits.
- Default palette: deep indigo, brick red, maize yellow, and warm cream. Restrained print texture belongs inside shapes. Avoid gradients, photorealistic modeling, dense scratchy detail, or distressed outer debris.
- Flowers and foliage feel deliberately drawn with a little asymmetry. Do not copy identifiable pottery patterns, existing folk artwork, or branded card layouts.
- Optional cream title cartouches with bold hand-cut serif lettering. Use only requested or clearly appropriate copy; verify spelling and accents. Branding is not automatic.

## Adaptation

Preserve the requested subject's anatomy and identifying details; do not carry Xolo ears or body proportions into other animals. Preserve the visual language rather than tracing the original scene. Let the requested mood and subject determine flora and palette changes. Do not introduce Mexican cultural claims or Indigenous symbols merely because the reference depicts a Mexican breed.

For tees and crewnecks, use a clear central panel. For hoodies, account for hood and pocket placement. For mugs, unfold the botanical frame into a wrap if useful. For wall art, allow richer border detail. For ornaments, redraw a simplified bust or silhouette with fewer flowers and minimal text rather than shrinking the panel.

## Execution

Use the available image-generation workflow for raster artwork. Specify the reference as a style reference and describe the new subject, composition, exact copy, palette, product constraints, and background treatment. Generate standalone artwork separately from mockups. Use real transparency for merchandise cutouts; composed backgrounds are appropriate for wall art when requested. Intentional cream ink should remain opaque.

Inspect subject anatomy, silhouette, text, and floral hierarchy. Do not call artwork print-ready until dimensions, transparency, edges, contrast, small details, and the provider's requirements have been checked. Save new variants without replacing approved originals. Follow the user's current approval workflow; this skill does not add a mandatory approval gate or authorize publication.
